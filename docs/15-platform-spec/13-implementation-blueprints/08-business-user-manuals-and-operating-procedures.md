# Business User Manuals & Standard Operating Procedures

**Covers:** BIZ-D01–BIZ-D09 · Draft framework; publish after actual product verification

## Document set
Separate manuals for Staff Portal, Technician App, Customer App and Customer Web Portal; SOPs for employee access, customer onboarding, business workflows, support/escalation and document management. Do not publish screenshots or instructions for unimplemented features.

## Staff Portal manual
Cover sign-in/MFA, dashboard, My Work, search, filters, record detail, forms, validation, follow-up, assignment, approvals, document access, reports and sign-out. Add task guides for sales, survey, design, project operations, procurement, finance, support and managers. State role boundaries and escalation route.

## Technician manual
Cover install/sign-in, job download, schedule, site verification, safety stop/escalation, checklist, evidence, serial scan, offline work, sync state, submit, reviewer feedback and lost-device reporting. App checklists do not replace technical training or competent supervision.

## Customer manual
Cover invitation, verification, profile, property, project timeline, quotation response, confirmed receipts, plant performance, data freshness, service request, notifications, support and privacy. Explain that estimated values and missing monitoring data are not confirmed production or faults.

## Employee onboarding SOP
Manager requests account; identity is verified; employee record is created; groups, permission set and scope assigned; MFA/device policy completed; training acknowledged. Review access on role change. On exit revoke sessions/access, transfer work ownership and preserve actor history.

## Customer onboarding SOP
Confirm customer/site record; verify contact through approved method; issue time-limited invitation; customer verifies and accepts notice; link only authorised sites; explain support/privacy; set language/channel preference. Expired/revoked invitations are safely reissued.

## Business process SOPs
Each SOP contains purpose, scope, owner, prerequisites, inputs, steps, approvals, evidence, exceptions, escalation, records, retention and review date. Required SOPs: enquiry qualification, survey, design review, quotation approval, project kickoff, procurement, goods receipt, receipt verification, installation readiness, commissioning, handover, alert triage, service case and closure.

## Support and document management
Support intake captures reporter, site/plant, impact, urgency, time, evidence and consent; checks duplicates; assigns owner; communicates reference/next update; escalates safety/security; records resolution. Document SOP classifies, templates, verifies source, reviews, issues immutable version, restricts access and applies retention/legal hold.

## Reporting definitions and publication gate
Every KPI has owner, formula, numerator/denominator, time basis, inclusions/exclusions, source and refresh. Separate leads/customers, issued/accepted quotes, claims/confirmed receipts, estimates/actuals and alerts/open cases. Publish manuals only after corresponding screens pass UAT; include version, supported app build, real screenshots, review date and support contact.
