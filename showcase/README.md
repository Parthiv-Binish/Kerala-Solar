# GreenGrid Solar Platform Showcase

Open [index.html](index.html) in a browser. This is a self-contained, responsive static website with no build step or third-party runtime dependency.

## What it explains

- The proposed connected platform and its three experiences: internal web workspace, customer app and technician app.
- The complete enquiry-to-customer-to-project-to-installation-to-service journey.
- Surveys, designs, quotations, utility tracking, procurement, inventory, work orders, accounts, cases, warranty and AMC.
- Monitoring and alert-to-service workflows, subject to confirmed equipment and manufacturer access.
- Group-based staff access, customer privacy, notifications and compliance boundaries.
- Indicative delivery phases, assumptions and open questions for GreenGrid.

## Status

Concept and study presentation only. It is not a deployed application or a statement that proposed integrations are live. See [the case study](../casestudy/README.md) and [the platform specification](../docs/15-platform-spec/README.md) for source detail.
