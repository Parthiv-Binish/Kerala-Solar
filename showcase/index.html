The idea

Solar work has many moving parts.
Customers should not have to chase them.

A solar installation involves conversations, site visits, designs, approvals, equipment, technicians, utility milestones and future service. The study brings those steps together so the right person can see what is happening and what needs to happen next.

01

Know what is next

Every enquiry, project, approval and service request has a clear status, responsible person and next action.

02

Keep everyone connected

Office staff, customers and technicians work from the same project information, with access suited to their work.

03

Keep a useful history

Documents, site photos, equipment serial numbers, updates and service records stay linked to the right customer and site.

A closer look · Enquiry to customer

Every enquiry gets a next step.

A new enquiry is not automatically a customer. The team first understands the requirement, follows up, surveys the site and prepares a proposal.

When a proposal is accepted and the required checks are complete, the team converts the enquiry into a customer/project. Existing customers can have more than one site or project.
01 · New

Capture the enquiry

Record contact details, source, site location, solar requirement and preferred contact method. Avoid collecting unnecessary identity documents at this stage.

02 · Contacted

Assign a staff member

A sales person receives ownership, contacts the prospect and records the conversation and next follow-up date.

03 · Qualified

Understand the requirement

Clarify residential, commercial or industrial use; on-grid, off-grid or hybrid needs; electricity usage; budget and site readiness.

04 · Survey & proposal

Visit, assess and prepare a quote

The survey captures roof area, shade, electrical details and photos. The proposal lists equipment, capacity, estimated generation, price, taxes, payment stages and warranty terms.

05 · Decision

Won, lost or on hold

Accepted proposals move forward. Declined opportunities keep a reason and history. On-hold enquiries get a planned follow-up.

06 · Conversion

Create the customer relationship

The accepted enquiry is linked to a customer, site and project. Duplicate checks prevent creating the same customer twice. App access is invited separately.

What the system helps GreenGrid do

Choose a part of the business to explore.

Instead of putting every feature on one screen, the platform groups work into clear steps. Open a section to see what staff, customers and technicians can do.

1. Find customers and manage enquiries

Keep every new customer enquiry in one place and make sure someone is responsible for the next step.

07

Capture enquiries

Add enquiries received by phone, website, WhatsApp, referrals or walk-ins. Keep contact details, location, requirements and enquiry source together.

08

Assign and follow up

Assign an employee, set a follow-up date, record conversations and see which enquiries are waiting for a response.

09

Prepare and track quotations

Record site survey findings, prepare a quotation, keep revised versions and track whether the customer accepted, declined or needs more time.

2. Manage projects from approval to handover

Once a customer accepts the proposal, the enquiry becomes a project with its own tasks, documents and progress history.

10

Keep project progress clear

Track each project stage, its owner, due date, pending reason and next action. One customer can have more than one site or project.

11

Track KSEB and scheme steps

Store application references, required documents, inspection updates, meter progress and subsidy status. Staff record verified updates; official portals remain authoritative.

12

Plan installation work

Create work orders, assign technicians, schedule visits and record checklists, equipment serial numbers, photos, readings and completion review.

3. Keep track of materials and payments

Give the office a clearer picture of what has been ordered, what is available and what has been paid.

13

Manage purchasing and stock

Record suppliers, purchase orders, received items, available stock, materials reserved for a project, returns and equipment serial numbers.

14

Record bills and payments

Track quotations, invoices, payment stages, receipts, pending balances and refunds. Payments can be recorded manually; an online payment gateway is optional.

15

Keep project documents together

Store surveys, quotations, approvals, installation photos, invoices, warranties and handover documents against the correct customer and project.

4. Support customers after installation

The relationship continues after the system is installed. Customers can get help and GreenGrid can keep a record of the work done.

16

Receive service requests

Customers can raise an issue, add details and follow the request. Staff can assign it, record updates and track how long it has been waiting.

17

Manage warranty and maintenance

Keep warranty details, service history, manufacturer claims and Annual Maintenance Contract visits and renewals linked to the solar plant.

18

View solar performance

Where the equipment supports a connection, show generation, device status and faults. A possible issue can be reviewed and turned into a technician visit.

5. Coordinate employees and keep people informed

Give each person the information they need and keep customers updated at important moments.

19

Organise staff into groups

Create groups such as Sales, Technicians, Accounts and Management. Employees can belong to more than one group, with access limited by their permissions and assigned work.

20

Manage approvals and reports

Set who can approve quotations, purchases or refunds. Management can review follow-ups, delayed projects, pending meter work, payments and team workload.

21

Send customer notifications

Send relevant updates through approved WhatsApp, email, app push notifications or compliant SMS, based on customer preferences and consent.

Examples of automatic updates: enquiry received, survey scheduled, quotation ready, installation date confirmed, project milestone changed, service request assigned, service completed and maintenance renewal approaching.

Know whether a message arrived

Keep a record of messages waiting to send, sent, delivered or failed. Where supported by the provider, record read status too.

Handle failed messages safely

Retry temporary failures, prevent duplicate messages, record delivery errors and allow authorised staff to resend when appropriate.

After commissioning

A solar plant should not become invisible after handover.

Where the installed equipment and manufacturer access support it, the platform can collect performance data and show it to authorised users.

Monitoring depends on the exact inverter, datalogger, firmware, connectivity and manufacturer API access. Brand names alone do not guarantee integration.
22

Performance view

Energy generated, current power, daily and lifetime totals, battery state and grid import/export where available.

23

Device health

Online/offline state, last update time, device faults and connection quality.

24

Useful alerts

Possible offline equipment, fault conditions or unusual output can be flagged using model-aware rules.

25

Alert to service

An alert can be reviewed and, when appropriate, turned into a service case and assigned to a technician.

Trust, safety & transparency

Technology should make the process clearer — not promise what it cannot control.

The platform supports the work and keeps evidence. Government authorities, manufacturers, qualified professionals and payment providers remain responsible for their own approvals and systems.

26

Government portals stay authoritative

The system records portal references, evidence and verified updates. It does not pretend to approve applications or replace KSEB, MNRE or other official systems.

27

Rules are checked and versioned

Scheme eligibility, capacity rules, metering, equipment compliance and tax treatment must be checked against current official requirements before quoting or applying.

28

Privacy and controlled access

Staff groups, record-level access, audit logs, consent preferences, data minimisation and secure document access are part of the design.

What about PM Surya Ghar and subsidy?

The study treats PM Surya Ghar as a residential rooftop solar programme with eligibility and process conditions. It is not a universal subsidy for every commercial, industrial, off-grid or solar-water-heater project. Current eligibility, CFA amounts, deadlines and process must be verified for each customer using the latest official guidance.

Will the system automatically connect to KSEB or E-Kiran?

No automatic integration is assumed. The study found no confirmed public third-party API for these portals. The initial design supports staff-entered status with reference numbers, timestamps and evidence. An official integration can be added only if access is formally authorised and documented.

Will every inverter brand show live generation?

No. It depends on the exact model, datalogger, firmware, customer account permissions and manufacturer API terms. The first supported device family should be selected only after GreenGrid confirms its installed equipment and access.

Are WhatsApp messages guaranteed?

No channel can guarantee delivery in every circumstance. The recommendation is to use the official WhatsApp Business Platform where permitted, with email, push and compliant SMS options. Consent, templates, provider status and delivery failures must be handled.

Does the platform issue electrical or statutory certificates?

No. It stores the evidence and documents issued by authorised people or authorities. It does not replace electrical engineering judgement, inspections, statutory certificates or professional sign-off.

How will employee access work?

Employees can belong to one or more groups, such as Sales, Technician, Accounts or Management. Group permissions are combined, but the system also checks whether a person is assigned to the job or allowed to view that branch or record. Sensitive finance and personal information can have additional restrictions.

A practical rollout

Start with the work that connects everything.

The study proposes staged delivery. The sequence and duration are planning estimates, not a committed delivery date.

Phase 0 · 1–2 weeks

Confirm how GreenGrid works

Meet the team, validate forms and documents, confirm roles, equipment brands, customer journey and current processes.

Phase 1 · 6–9 weeks

Build the operational core

Staff access, enquiries, customers, surveys, quotations, projects, utility tracking, documents, cases and notifications.

Phase 2 · 4–6 weeks

Connect the field and customer

Customer and technician apps, offline work, inventory, procurement, warranty, AMC and deeper finance/reporting.

Phase 3 · 4–8 weeks

Add plant monitoring

Confirm equipment access, build the first supported device connector, dashboards and alert-to-case flow.

Phase 4 · 2–3 weeks

Test, train and launch

Security checks, backup restoration, pilot, staff training, issue fixing and production readiness review.

Planning estimate

About 17–28 weeks

Indicative small-team estimate. Client decisions, integrations, migration and regulatory review may change the timeline.

Indicative infrastructure planning: ₹1,100–₹3,250 per month in the study, excluding development effort, messaging usage, provider fees, taxes, manufacturer access and app-store fees. This is an estimate, not a supplier quote.
